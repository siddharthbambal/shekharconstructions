THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE VISION

Different strengths. A shared commitment to creating better places

### Chandrashekhar Bambal

**Co-Founder · Civil Engineer**

With 28 years in construction and development, Chandrashekhar is a familiar and respected presence in Nagpur’s real-estate community. His experience extends well beyond the city, with travels across more than 70 countries shaping a broad perspective on people, places and possibilities.

> **“The longer you work in this field, the more you realise — buildings are only part of what we leave behind. The real legacy is the trust people place in you.”**

### Ar. Kanchan Bambal

**Founder · Architect · Design Leadership**

With over 28 years in architecture and planning, Kanchan brings a rich mix of professional experience, creative perspective and a life shaped by exposure to different cultures and places. Her work and leadership have earned her numerous awards and recognitions, while her journey has also taken her to the **Miss Worldwide competition in Greece as a finalist**.

> **“I’ve always believed that good architecture has a personality. It should have a sense of place, but also a sense of the people it is made for.”**

### Ar. Siddharth Bambal

**Architect · Design & Innovation**

An architecture graduate from **Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur**, Siddharth brings a new-generation perspective to the practice. Having travelled across more than 60 countries, he draws from a wide range of architectural influences. His thesis project, a **corporate headquarters for Zoho**, was recognised by the **Indian Institute of Architects (IIA)** during his academic journey.

> **“Architecture is changing quickly. I think our job is not to predict exactly what comes next, but to design spaces that are ready for it.”**


DIFFERENT STRENGTHS. ONE DIRECTION.

Experience to build with confidence. Architecture to live with meaning. And a new perspective to take it forward.

Together, they bring construction, architecture and forward-thinking design under one vision.
