Material Quality & Engineering

Premium Building Specifications

Constructed with high-grade materials, precision structural RCC framing, and branded fittings throughout.

Structure & Walls

  • Earthquake resistant RCC frame structure designed by registered structural engineers.
  • High-strength AAC / fly-ash block masonry for superior thermal insulation.
  • Double-coat sand face exterior plaster with weather-shield acrylic paint.
  • Smooth gypsum/POP finish with premium plastic emulsion paint internally.

Flooring & Tiling

  • Grand 800mm x 1600mm / 4x2 premium glazed vitrified tiles in living, dining & bedrooms.
  • Anti-skid designer tiles in all attached balconies and dry terrace.
  • Full-height luxury designer ceramic tiles in all bathrooms up to ceiling level.
  • Granite stone framing for all window sills and bathroom door jambs.

Bathrooms & Plumbing

  • Premium concealed CPVC plumbing with Jaquar / Kohler CP fittings.
  • Wall-hung sanitary commodes with concealed flushing cisterns.
  • Hot and cold water diverters in all master bathrooms.
  • Solar water heater connection provision & geyser points.

Electrical & Cabling

  • Concealed copper wiring (Polycab / Finolex / RR Kabel) with MCBs.
  • Modular switches (Legrand / Schneider / Anchor Roma).
  • AC points in all bedrooms and living room.
  • TV, high-speed fiber internet and intercom conduits provided.

Doors, Windows & Security

  • Designer laminated main door with digital smart lock & video door phone provision.
  • Heavy-section powder-coated aluminum sliding windows with mosquito mesh.
  • Granite kitchen platform with stainless steel sink & exhaust fan provision.

Common Amenities
