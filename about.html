Core Leadership

Three Generations of Vision & Expertise

Co-Founder · Civil Engineer

Chandrashekhar Bambal

With 28 years in construction and development, Chandrashekhar is a familiar and highly respected presence in Nagpur's real-estate community. His experience extends well beyond the city, with travels across more than 70 countries shaping a broad perspective on people, structural durability, and the future of urban infrastructure.

“The longer you work in this field, the more you realise — buildings are only part of what we leave behind. The real legacy is the trust people place in you.”
Founder · Architect · Design Leadership

Ar. Kanchan Bambal

With over 28 years in architecture and planning, Kanchan brings a rich mix of professional experience, creative perspective, and an international worldview shaped by exposure to diverse cultures. Her work and leadership have earned numerous awards from MAREDCO, NAREDCO, CREDAI, and IIA, while her personal journey also took her to the prestigious Miss Worldwide competition in Greece as a finalist.

“I’ve always believed that good architecture has a personality. It should have a sense of place, but also a sense of the people it is made for.”
Architect · Design & Innovation

Ar. Siddharth Bambal

An architecture graduate from the premier Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur. Having traveled across more than 60 countries, Siddharth brings an international perspective to high-density vertical living, energy-responsive building skins, and smart materials. His academic thesis, a corporate headquarters for Zoho, was recognized by the Indian Institute of Architects (IIA).

“Architecture is changing quickly. I think our job is not to predict exactly what comes next, but to design spaces that are ready for it.”
