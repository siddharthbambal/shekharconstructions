Our Journey

Building with experience.
Growing with every milestone.

What began as a single practice in 1998 has evolved across decades into a multidisciplinary infrastructure leader spanning construction, architecture, development, and interiors.

1998–1999 Foundation

18 Homes. One Beginning.

Chandrashekhar Bambal launched his practice taking on 18 bungalows along Koradi Road — laying the foundation for decades of architectural excellence.

18 Bungalows in Year 01
2000–2007 Building a Reputation

From Homes to Landmark Projects

With Kanchan Bambal joining the family practice and partnering with SK Group of Architects, Shekhar Construction gained renown across North Nagpur for ahead-of-their-time designs.

150+ Projects completed including bungalows, schools, colleges, resorts, and politician Chandrashekhar Bawankule's residence.
2008–2012 Development & Neighbourhoods

Shaping Vidyanagar's Skyline

Beginning with our landmark Siddhi Siddharth Apartment (2008), Bambal Infrastructure transformed Vidyanagar within 4 years with 4 major commercial & residential developments.
