How a focused construction practice in Nagpur grew into a multidisciplinary infrastructure brand delivering over 1,000 landmark developments.

1998–1999 Foundation

18 Bungalows. One Bold Beginning.

Civil engineer Chandrashekhar Bambal launched the practice by taking on 18 private bungalows along Koradi Road — setting down bedrock principles of personal supervision, structural honesty, and client trust that guide us to this day.

18 Bungalows in Year 01
2000–2007 Building a Reputation

From Homes to Landmark Institutional Projects

With Ar. Kanchan Bambal joining the family practice and formalizing SK Group of Architects, Shekhar Constructions gained renown across North Nagpur for ahead-of-their-time designs and solid engineering.

150+ Completed projects including luxury bungalows, schools, colleges, resorts, and the private residence of Honorable Chandrashekhar Bawankule (Cabinet Minister of Revenue of Maharashtra).
