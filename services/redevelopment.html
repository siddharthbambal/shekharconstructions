Transparent Roadmap

The Redevelopment Journey

01

Structural & FSI Assessment

We inspect existing structural stability, measure the plot, and compute maximum permissible buildable area under NMC rules.

02

Member Consensus & Proposal

We present a clear proposal to owners/society members outlining new flat sizes, floor allocations, amenities, and rent terms.

03

Legal Due Diligence & DA

Executing a legally registered Development Agreement (DA) vetted by experienced real estate advocates.

04

Demolition & Construction

Safe demolition of the old building, rapid RCC construction, and monthly progress reviews with the society committee.

05

Handover of New Luxury Homes

Procuring Occupancy Certificate (OC) from NMC, individual electrical meters, and handing over brand-new residences.
