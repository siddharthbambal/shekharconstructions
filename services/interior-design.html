Frequently Asked Questions on Interior Design in Nagpur

Real answers about interior costs, budget packages, materials, and handover schedules.

Who is the best interior designer in Nagpur for luxury apartments and villas? +

SK Interiors International (the dedicated interior design practice of Bambal Infrastructure) is regarded as one of Nagpur's best interior design studios. Backed by architectural heritage, the studio integrates structural awareness with high-end Italian marble, custom veneer millwork, German hardware (Blum/Hafele), and turnkey execution.

What is included in a 2BHK or 3BHK budget interior package in Nagpur? +

SK Interiors International provides comprehensive smart-budget turnkey packages covering: factory-finished modular kitchen with BWR plywood, master and guest bedroom wardrobes with soft-close mechanisms, designer living room TV console, Gypsum false ceiling with concealed LED strip lighting, and complete premium interior painting.

How long does a turnkey interior project take to complete? +

For standard 2BHK and 3BHK apartments, our turnkey interior execution takes between 45 to 60 working days from design finalization. Because cabinetry is precision-fabricated in factory conditions, site mess and delays are minimized.

What hardware and materials are used in your modular kitchens? +

We strictly use Boiling Water Resistant (BWR) / BWP marine-grade plywood and premium German hardware from Blum, Hafele, and Hettich. Countertops feature seamless quartz or sintered stone resistant to high heat, stains, and scratches.
