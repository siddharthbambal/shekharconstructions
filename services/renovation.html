Comprehensive Renovation

Transforming Outdated Spaces into Modern Landmarks

Whether you have an ancestral bungalow needing modern bathrooms and an open kitchen, or a commercial office requiring a layout overhaul, we deliver full-scope renovation.

Wall Removal & Open Layouts

  • Removing non-loadbearing dividing walls to create bright open-plan living-dining spaces.
  • Structural steel/RCC beam insertion to safely open up cramped rooms.
  • Relocating entryways, enlarging window openings, and bringing in natural daylight.

Complete Kitchen & Bath Overhaul

  • Full strip-down of leaking, dated plumbing lines and rusted cast-iron pipes.
  • Modern concealed CPVC diverters, wall-hung sanitaryware, and anti-leak water barriers.
  • New modular island kitchens, quartz countertops, and dedicated pantry storage.

Exterior Elevation Modernization

  • Modernizing traditional bungalow facades with textured cladding and metal pergolas.
  • Replacing leaking wooden windows with sound-insulating UPVC sliding systems.
  • External weather-proof crack filling, damp treatment, and designer exterior lighting.
