How a Land Joint Venture Works with Bambal

You bring the land. We bring 28 years of construction expertise, architectural innovation, municipal sanction liaisoning, and construction capital.

1. Legal & FSI Assessment

We verify land title documents, calculate maximum buildable square footage under current Unified DCPR regulations, and assess target buyer demand for that specific micro-market.

2. Registered Agreement

We execute a transparent Joint Development Agreement (JDA) clearly allocating the landowner's units (specific flats, floor numbers, and parking bays) before any earth is broken.

3. End-to-End Delivery & Sales

Bambal Infrastructure funds 100% of construction, oversees quality, acquires MahaRERA registration, and provides dedicated sales channels for monetizing your shared units.
