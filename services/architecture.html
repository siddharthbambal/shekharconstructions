Core Capabilities

What We Deliver for Your Plot

Comprehensive architectural services covering every stage from conceptual feasibility to municipal sanction and structural coordination.

Concept & Floor Plan Design

  • Optimal zoning of private, semi-private, and entertainment spaces.
  • Zero corridor wastage ensuring high carpet-to-builtup ratio.
  • Scientific Vastu compliance integrated naturally into modern aesthetics.
  • Solar orientation, natural cross-ventilation, and thermal comfort modeling.

FSI & Development Viability

  • Complete plot assessment under latest Unified DCPR Maharashtra rules.
  • Maximizing permissible FSI, TDR loading, and premium FSI consumption.
  • Height, setback, and parking ratio calculations under municipal bylaws.
  • Feasibility reports comparing individual bungalows vs flat scheme models.

3D Elevation & Visualizations

  • Photorealistic 3D architectural renders of exterior facades and night lighting.
  • Isometric 3D floor plan cutaways showing furnished interior volumes.
  • Material palette selection (stone cladding, louvers, metal trims, texture paint).
  • Virtual 360-degree architectural walkthrough previews for clients.

Sanction Drawings & Approvals

  • Preparation of official sanction drawings for NMC & NMRDA building permissions.
  • Fire NOC, water connection, and environmental clearance coordination.
  • Commencement Certificate (CC) and Occupancy Certificate (OC) processing.
  • MahaRERA layout documentation and registered architect certification.

Working Drawings & Coordination

  • Detailed 1:20 and 1:10 architectural working construction drawings for site.
  • Direct coordination with structural engineers for beam/column placement.
  • Integrated MEP drawings (plumbing risers, electrical conduits, HVAC sleeves).
  • Periodic site inspection visits to verify that construction matches design.

Specialized Building Types

  • Custom luxury private bungalows and weekend farmhouses.
  • Multi-storey residential apartment schemes & twin towers.
  • Hospitals, specialized surgical clinics & diagnostic facilities.
  • Schools, engineering colleges & institutional auditoriums.
How We Work

The 5-Stage Architectural Design Workflow

A structured, disciplined process ensuring clarity, no surprises on site, and timely municipal clearances.

01

Client Brief & Site Analysis

We study your plot orientation, soil profile, surrounding views, road width, and family lifestyle brief.

02

Conceptual Space Planning

Developing floor layouts, circulation paths, room dimensions, and initial 3D massing studies.

03

3D Elevation & Material Selection

Refining facade identity, window fenestrations, balcony treatments, and evening illumination effects.

04

Municipal Sanctions (NMC/NMRDA)

Preparing compliant sanction sheets, liaisoning with municipal town planning, and obtaining official Building Permits.

05

Working Drawings & Site Supervision

Issuing millimeter-precise execution drawings and conducting milestone site quality inspections.
