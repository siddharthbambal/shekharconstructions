How do you maximize FSI under Maharashtra UDCPR rules? +

Under Maharashtra's Unified DCPR (UDCPR), total buildable potential is determined by road width, plot area, and land use. We calculate the exact matrix of Basic FSI, Premium FSI, and TDR (Transferable Development Rights), while loading up to 60% Ancillary FSI for residential (or 80% for commercial). Along the Nagpur Metro corridor, we also utilize Transit-Oriented Development (TOD) FSI enhancements.

What approvals are required for building construction in Nagpur? +

Construction projects require official Building Plan Sanctions from either Nagpur Municipal Corporation (NMC) for city areas or Nagpur Metropolitan Region Development Authority (NMRDA) for outer regions. Depending on your land history, you may also need a Release Letter (RL) from NIT, Gunthewari regularization, Fire NOC, and MahaRERA registration. SK Group of Architects manages this end-to-end.

Can you design low-cost or budget homes in Nagpur? +

Yes. We offer specialized value-engineering for cost-effective residential housing. By standardizing structural column spans for repetitive shuttering, eliminating corridor waste to achieve 75%+ carpet efficiency, and specifying durable regional materials like AAC blocks and fly-ash bricks, we cut construction costs by 20% to 25% without sacrificing durability.
